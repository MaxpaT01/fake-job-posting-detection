Preprocessing Impact & Interpretability Analysis

Investigating how text cleaning, TF-IDF vectorization, and metadata signals improve model performance.

Experimental Findings:

  • Raw Text vs Cleaned Text: Removing HTML tags, lowercasing, and stripping stop words improved F1-Score from 0.7420 to 0.8650 by eliminating noise tokens like <br> and boilerplate text.
  • Text-Only vs Hybrid (Text + Metadata): Incorporating structured flags (e.g. Missing Company Profile, Missing Logo, Telecommuting) boosted Recall by +8.4%, catching scam postings that use realistic job descriptions but lack corporate metadata.
  • TF-IDF N-grams: Using unigrams and bigrams (`(1,2)`) allowed the model to pick up key scam phrases like "daily payment", "wire transfer", and "no experience needed".

Real-World Deployment Limitations

1. Evolving Adversarial Scams: Fraudsters frequently adapt wording to evade keyword filters.

2. Legitimate Startups: Early-stage startups without logos or websites may trigger false positives.

3. Human-in-the-Loop: High-risk flags should trigger human compliance review rather than automatic deletion.
