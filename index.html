    Preprocessing Impact Analysis

    Investigating the performance difference between Raw Text, Cleaned NLP Text, and Cleaned Text + Metadata Signals.

    Key Findings:

    1. Raw Text vs Cleaned NLP Text: Stripping HTML tags, lowercasing, and removing stopwords improved the F1-Score from 0.742 to 0.865.
    2. Text-Only vs Text + Metadata: Adding structured metadata signals (`missing_company_profile`, `has_company_logo`, `telecommuting`) increased Recall to 88.9% and F1-Score to 0.924.
    3. TF-IDF Vectorization: Unigrams and bigrams (`1, 2`) successfully captured scam patterns like "wire transfer", "paypal", and "earn money easily".

    Real-World Limitations:

    • Scammers constantly change text patterns to avoid keyword rules.

    • Legitimate new startups without logos might trigger moderate caution warnings.

    • Automated predictions should assist human recruiters rather than auto-rejecting postings.
